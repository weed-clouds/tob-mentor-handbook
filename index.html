MAIDEN

At door:

  • Everyone drops salve, gets spec gear ready.
  • Maiden has a real spec order, but learner inventories vary; don't assume everyone owns hammer, bgs, or ralos. Goal for this raid: 0 defence.
  • Everyone with hammer sends it. BGS havers watch Party Def Tracker and back up if Maiden isn't at 0 def.
  • Mentor handles backup BGS in this raid.
  • Mages: enter, throw hammer, swap back into range gear.

mage / freezes — show freeze image

  • Explain freeze rolls for N/S.
  • Go over gear switches for freezing; Augury on.
  • Be off Maiden at least 5% early to get into position.
  • Crabs pop at at 70%, 50%, 30%.
  • Once all crabs are frozen attack the clump and pay attention to boss health.
  • After 30s put on range and attack boss. Can chally last hit if wanting to.

melee / range — show aggro image

  • Explain Maiden aggro and who is taking autos.
  • range stand south, mdps stands north
  • Watch teammates' HP; step up to tank if someone needs it.
  • Move after every 2 Maiden attacks in case blood spawns.
  • Maiden autos drain stats. Watch boosts and repot with extra super combats.
  • Crabs: only south1 and north1 priorities; never clump. Sometimes s2 if out of clump.

Leaving maiden

  • Check stats / repot.
  • Pick up dropped item(s).
  • Eat angler for Bloat.

BLOAT

At door:

  • Check boosts / repot.
  • EQUIP SALVE.
  • No need for 0 KC learners to risk a BGS spec.

movement / prayers

  • Protect from Ranged at all time while inside; Piety at least while attacking. Feel free to leave it on.
  • Bloat can walk, run or turn around. Don't wait in place too long.
  • Stay on opposite side of pillar; hug pillar between downs — standing one tile off can get you hit by flies when he rounds corners.
  • Dodge falling hands/feet while moving.
  • On every down, after attacking move to the closest corner side so you have a fast escape; run around the nearest corner afterward to escape.

down spec orders

  • First down (chally): chally → 4 whip hits → chally → run.
  • you can step back 1 tile after the 4th whip to get closer to the corner as u chally before leaving
  • if u have claws can squeeze in a 5th whip if you are early
  • Second down: around 5 whip hits → chally → run.
  • Don't force a two-down. Staying alive matters more for learners.

leaving bloat / chest

  • Drop salves for space.
  • Buy hard food as needed.
  • Low-agility melees can buy a stamina potion for Nylo.

NYLOCAS

Waves:

  • Explain Nyloer and confirm each learner has styles set correctly.
  • Melees pick east or west and stay on that side; fill south when needed. Don't follow each other around the room.
  • Explain doubles: mage can barrage; melee/range can later learn wave numbers and use chins/chally/scythe as doubles enter.
  • Before wave 20: focus smalls first. After wave 20: focus bigs first.

boss:

  • Make sure you’re potted for boss;
  • If you have a bgs feel free to swing it on the first swing and then whip after for your first two attacks.
  • If you have claws u can send them 1st as well, if not you can send a chally on your first and last attacks.
  • Always starts melee; each style lasts 2 boss attacks, then changes randomly.
  • PRAYER FIRST, GEAR SECOND. Match your protection prayer and weapon/style before attacking.
  • As a starting point: aim for 2 hits per style; 3 blowpipe/ayak hits, if on time.
  • Don't hit off-style — you will heal the boss and regret that shit.
  • Range pot before entering.

SOTETSEG

positions / specs

  • Point out the tile packs and where to stand
  • Point out spec order on ground. And show them how that lines up with there orb order
  • Remember your Sote position — same position carries to Xarpus and Verzik.
  • Equip fang if brought.

fight

  • Orbs come quickly: red = pray mage; black = pray range.
  • If chosen for ball, stack on the middle tile.
  • Boss can melee for 45 off prayer — pay attention if in melee distance.

maze

  • Step on tile 1; wait on tile 3.
  • Take your time and click the red tiles.
  • If you mess up, KEEP GOING. NEVER TURN BACK.

chest after sote

  • Sip range potion, then drop it for space.
  • Fill supplies for Verzik: at least 3 restores, remaining space brews/hard food.
  • Teammates should share supplies if needed.

XARPUS

positions / exhumeds / specs

  • Reconfirm positions from Sote.
  • P1 exhumeds: cover/fill your assigned quadrant.
  • Spec from the correct middle tile; hammer first, then BGS/Ralos if needed.
  • After specs, run to wall and equip full range + blowpipe.

poison phase

  • Attack from wall until poison is visibly about to land on your tile, then move 2 tiles.
  • It can come from both the boss itself or be bounced to you by others. Just keep an eye out.
  • Keep it simple — don't overcomplicate the poison movement.

post-screech

  • After screech: switch to whip or stay BP; move into attack tiles. (Tile packs)
  • Boss turns every 8 ticks.
  • Never attack while Xarpus is looking at you — the damage is reflected and you will regret that shit.
  • Can fit around 2 whip hits or 3–4 BP hits if on time.
  • Wait for boss to turn, attack while moving toward the side it is looking at; after next turn, move to that side and repeat. Only attack when it isn't looking at you.

VERZIK P1

staff / pillars

  • Explain staff orb/spec order and dropping/passing staff.
  • Watch who has staff and who is next.
  • Get 2 staff specs then drop staff behind pillar.
  • Protect from Magic at all times (off-prayer attack can hit 99).
  • Offensive prayers do not matter
  • First cycle: 4 whip hits then pillar. After: 2 whip hits then pillar.
  • Melee damage is capped at 10 per hitsplat, even with scythe (17) staff specs (75-150) matter more than missed melee swings.
  • Missing a swing or skipping a cycle for safety is fine. Taking repeated Verzik hits is not.
  • Only limited time outside pillar; do not panic or overstay trying to get a melee hit.
  • MAKE SURE YOU SPEC TWICE AND DROP THE STAFF IMMEDIATELY BEHIND THE PILLAR

VERZIK P2

p2 sim

  • show p2 whip-walk in sim.
  • Use whip for all of p2 (fang can be used later in p3/webs/tanking).
  • Watch cape flick / listen for sound → click Verzik to attack → walk back.
  • Try not to run too far away (zap); try to stay around 50–60+ HP, especially on crabs.
  • Protect from Ranged for all p2 before reds.

crabs

  • Spawn is random. If your true tile touches crab radius marker, it explodes if it’s your crab. If you walk under the center it will auto explode for up to 63 dmg.
  • East side → run south. West side → run north.
  • Eat up in p2 in case crabs come and you get bounced; prioritize survival.
  • When crabs come out a purple crab will spawn with them that must be hit 1 time with poison [bp/tent whip]. If you land any damage stop attacking it. Green animation.
  • reds at 35%
  • Switch immediately to Protect from Magic when reds spawn at 35%.
  • While red crabs are out, attacking Verzik heals her.
  • Hit reds below 40% HP, then return to boss.
  • Once the boss is around 10%, if a new group of reds pop, do not hit more than twice before getting on boss to skip.

VERZIK P3

tank / autos

  • Tank is chosen randomly. Don't panic.
  • For learners, 1:1 tanking: stand centered under Verzik → see attack animation → click boss to step out and attack → immediately step back under → repeat.
  • Obligatory P3 sim 1:1 — Verzik P3 Trainer
  • Be centered under her body so Verzik doesn't move one tile and uncover you.
  • When you're under for her attack, she can't melee the team.
  • Autos: blue = mage; green = range.

crabs → webs

  • After the first 4 p3 autos, crabs spawn; pop them without dragging over tank.
  • Everyone moves south for webs; show web tiles.
  • Webs start 4 attacks after crabs.
  • If you miss web starting position: keep moving around perimeter, don't stop and get webbed.

yellows → green ball

  • After webs, damage boss until yellows; take the closest yellow and heal while standing on it if needed.
  • Green ball comes after yellows; it hits 74 — have more than 74 HP beforehand.
  • Can pass ball by following another player to reduce damage

20% / tornadoes

  • At 20%, tornadoes spawn; boss changes 7-tick → 5-tick attack cycle.
  • Tank should run away instead of staying under.
  • Dodge purple tornado — they take half of your given hp and heal Verzik for 3× damage dealt (e.g. 99 hp is 49dmg x3 = 147 healing).
  • Once purples spawn, send claw specs if brought. If not, chally spec for what you think is the last swing.
